The Letter

The garden still calls my name.

Prisca.

I know every path, where flowers are nurtured, where laughter lives.

That is what makes leaving hard.

Yet an eagle keeps crossing the same sky, dropping the same letter at my feet, as if God keeps sending the same message over and over again.

Beyond the gate awaits a crew of light, a future I am afraid to enter, a life I have not fully lived.

And in the distance, I hear:

Adewonuola.

A name the garden never knew, yet it sounds like home...

💻 My Journey Into Front-End Development

My journey into front-end development started in a very simple.
My younger brother was the first person who really introduced me to the world of technology.
He would tell me different things about tech, what people could do with it, and how technology was becoming such a big part of the world.
At first, I was just listening. I did not think I would actually become interested in it.
Then I found an opportunity to learn more about technology, and I decided to take it.
I was studying English Language at the time, so tech was not exactly what people would expect me to be doing.
But I have always been interested in trying different things and learning new skills, so I decided to give it a chance.
I started with the basics of front-end development. I learned about HTML, CSS and JavaScript and slowly began to understand how websites are created.
At first, even the smallest things could be confusing. Sometimes I would write code and wonder why nothing was working.
Other times, I would change one small thing and suddenly my whole page would look different. 😂
But that was also what made it interesting.
I started enjoying the process of taking an idea in my head and turning it into something people could actually see and use.
I loved changing colours, arranging sections, creating buttons and watching everything come together.
Being an English Language student has also made this journey interesting because I have realised that technology and language are not as different as they seem.
I enjoy writing, storytelling and communication, while technology gives me another way to express those things.
I am still learning. Front-end development is only the beginning for me.
I want to explore more areas of technology, including product design, back-end development, data, cybersecurity and artificial intelligence.
I do not know exactly where this journey will take me yet, but I know that taking that first opportunity was one of the best decisions I made.
Sometimes, all you need is someone to introduce you to something new and the courage to say, “Let me try.”

🌷 Writing Poetry In A Busy World

The world is always moving.
There is always something happening.
People are working, studying, rushing somewhere, talking, scrolling, planning their next move and trying to keep up with everything around them.
Sometimes, I think we forget to notice the small things.
For me, poetry is a way of slowing down.
I find beauty in little things.
A quiet morning.
The way light falls through a window.
A conversation that stays in your mind long after it ends.
The feeling of leaving something familiar.
A person smiling without knowing how much that smile meant.
Sometimes, I experience something and I immediately want to put it into words.
I think that is one of the beautiful things about writing.
You can see something that looks ordinary to everyone else, but to you, it means something more.
Poetry gives you a way to explain that feeling.
It does not always have to be complicated.
Sometimes, simple words can carry the biggest emotions.
I write because there are things I see and feel that I cannot always explain in normal conversation.
So I turn them into poems, stories or plays.
I try to take the beauty I see in the world and put it into a form that someone else can understand.
Maybe someone will read my words and realise that they have felt the same thing too.
That is what I love about poetry.
It reminds us that even in a busy world, there is still beauty worth noticing.
